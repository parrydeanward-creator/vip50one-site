# Product films: voiceover scripts

One script per film. Each line starts at its time and should finish inside its length (about 2.5 words a second). Each film also has its own .srt with the same lines and times.

**Voice:** the same voice as the ONE film: warm, confident, unhurried. Say "ONE" as the word one.

**Music:** the same family as the ONE film, so the six feel like a set: modern, hopeful, 95-105 BPM, ducked about 10 dB under the voice. A small lift on each new area; resolve on "Part of ONE" and hold 2 seconds.
